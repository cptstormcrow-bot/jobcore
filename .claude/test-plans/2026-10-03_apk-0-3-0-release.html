JobCore Android app, version 0.3.0

Release date of this plan: 2026-10-03. File to install: jobcore-0.3.0-vc5.apk.

How to use this plan. Read this page on your PC while the app runs on your phone or tablet. On each step, tap Pass, Fail or Not tested. If something looks wrong, type what you saw in the comment box under that step. When you finish, press Copy results at the bottom and paste the text back to Claude. Your answers are kept on this page if you close it.

Already checked for you

  • The installable app file was signed and verified by the build script.
  • The version numbers in the documents were swept and brought up to date.
  • The database changes for this release are already tracked and live.
  • The sales menu has six entries and five data screens that exist in the app (Pitch Picker is checked only as a menu entry):Overview, Review Library, Composer, Reporting and Callback tasks.
  • The Field Tech basics manual gained a chapter called Prem Jobs: Claim and Record (checked on disk; it is not a screen in the app).

Not tested in this release (carried to the next build's plan)

  • The five sales data screens, Overview, Review Library, Composer, Reporting and Callback tasks, filling with data in this release. The Recent reviews row check (step 11.3) is Not tested when they show empty.
  • The Prems button changing from Claim to Record on its own after a claim.
  • Which jobs fill the Next Job and Previous Jobs lists.
  • Queued offline edits syncing on start or refresh.
  • Internal changes with no screen impact.

After a Fail. After you mark a step Fail, go on to the next step. If the screen the next step needs cannot be reached, mark the rest of that test Not tested and go on to the next test.

1. Install and first open

Start here: your phone or tablet, with the old JobCore still installed and the install file jobcore-0.3.0-vc5.apk already copied onto the device. You need your admin login.

  1. On the device, tap the file jobcore-0.3.0-vc5.apk, allow the install when your phone asks, then open JobCore. When the first screen appears, sign in with your admin login.

    You should now see: the first screen titled Sign In with a Sign in button; after signing in, the app opens with the title Admin & Finance and Overview under it.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Tap the three-line menu button at the top left, then tap the gear next to the JobCore© chip. Scroll to the very bottom of the sheet titled Configuration.

    You should now see: a greyed row reading exactly Version 0.3.0 (build 5).

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  3. With the Configuration sheet still open, tap Settings.

    You should now see: a screen titled Settings with Configuration under it, showing the text Version 0.3.0 (build 5).

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: the app is installed, you are signed in, and the Version 0.3.0 (build 5) text was checked in both places.

2. Admin: logo menu and Review

Start here: signed in with your admin login, on the Admin & Finance home screen.

  1. Tap the three-line menu button at the top left, then tap the JobCore© chip at the top of the menu.

    You should now see: a module list with exactly three entries: Admin & Finance (highlighted), Field Tech and Sales & Marketing. The list does not include an entry called Payment & Review.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Tap the JobCore© chip again to get back to the normal screen list, then tap Review.

    You should now see: the Review screen, with the title Admin & Finance and Review under it.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: you have seen the module list with Admin & Finance, Field Tech and Sales & Marketing but no Payment & Review entry, and reached Review from the Admin & Finance list.

3. Admin: Overview cards

Start here: signed in with your admin login, on a phone held upright (on a tablet, mark both steps Not tested). Tap the three-line menu button, then tap Overview, under Admin & Finance.

  1. Look at the top of the Overview screen, directly under the title bar.

    You should now see: the first card, Leads, sitting directly under the title bar, and the gap between the title bar and the Leads card is no taller than the gap between two neighbouring cards.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Without scrolling, look for all 11 cards.

    You should now see: all 11 cards at once, in this order: Leads, Jobs, Assignments, Close-Outs, Price Sheets, Payments & Payouts, Review, Time off, Calendar, Reports, Employees. Mark Pass only if you needed no scrolling at all. If you had to scroll, mark Fail and type the name of the first card that was cut off in the comment box.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: you have checked the gap above Leads and whether all 11 cards, from Leads to Employees, fit without scrolling.

4. Admin: Employee detail

Start here: signed in with your admin login. Tap the three-line menu button, tap Employees, then tap the employee named Dev Tech in the Employee Roster list. If Dev Tech is not in the list, open any other employee and say so in the comment box.

  1. Scroll the whole employee screen from top to bottom.

    You should now see: the employee's name as the title, a details card that includes the rows Role and Hours Worked, then a Skills section and a Service Areas section. There is no schedule section and no job-history list on this screen.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Find the Skills heading.

    You should now see: each skill as a small rounded chip. Mark Pass only when you see chips. If you see the words No skills on file for this employee. instead, go back to the Employee Roster list and open another employee; if no employee shows chips, mark Not tested.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  3. Find the Hours Worked row in the details card. It shows three lines of plain text, This week, This month and This year. Read the three figures and type them into the comment box.

    You should now see: three hours figures. Mark Pass only when the week figure is no more than the month figure, the month figure is no more than the year figure, and at least one of the three differs from the others. If all three are 0, or all three are equal and not 0, mark Not tested.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  4. Scroll to the top and tap the Back button on the screen. Then open the same employee again and press your phone's own back button or back gesture.

    You should now see: both times you land on the Employee Roster list, not out of Admin & Finance.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: you have checked the sections, Skills, the three Hours Worked numbers, and both ways of going back to the Employee Roster.

5. Admin: Assignments and the title bar

Start here: signed in with your admin login, on your phone or tablet held upright. Tap the three-line menu button, then tap Assignments.

  1. Look at the top of the screen, then at the board.

    You should now see: the screen title sits fully below the phone's clock and battery row and nothing is cut off, and the board fits the screen width with no sideways scrolling. The cards titled Jobs and Employees are both fully visible.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Look at three screens in turn: Assignments; then the three-line menu button and Overview; then the three-line menu button and Employees.

    You should now see: on each of the three screens, no small rounded badge floating over the content near the bottom edge and reading Syncing… in grey, a number followed by pending in amber, or Sync failed — tap to retry in red.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  3. Go back to Assignments. In the Jobs card find the job "Drywall patch and paint touch-up". On its row, tap the dropdown that shows Unassigned with a small down arrow, tap Dev Tech, then tap Confirm. Then tap the three-line menu button, tap Overview, then the menu button and Assignments again.

    You should now see: the row for that job reading Tech: Dev Tech. If that job is not in the list or is already assigned, use any other job in the list; if there is no job to use, or Dev Tech is not in the dropdown, mark Not tested.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  4. On the same job row in the Jobs card, tap the dropdown, tap Unassigned, then tap Confirm. The dropdown has no Cancel button: if you open it by mistake, close it by tapping outside it or with the phone's back control. If step 5.3 was Not tested, mark this step Not tested too. If Unassigned is not offered, leave the job as it is, mark this step Not tested, and note that the job stays assigned to Dev Tech.

    You should now see: the row for that job no longer reading Tech: Dev Tech, with its dropdown showing Unassigned again.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: you have seen the title bar position, the missing corner badge on three screens, the assignment from Confirm still shown after leaving Assignments and coming back, and the job back on Unassigned (or marked Not tested).

6. Admin: Assign picker on a tablet

Start here: tablet only (on a phone, mark Not tested). Signed in with your admin login, tablet held upright. Tap the three-line menu button, then tap Assignments. You need a job in the Jobs card.

  1. Pick any job in the Jobs card. On its row, tap the dropdown with the small down arrow (it shows Unassigned or a tech's name). Look at the longest name in the list. If every name fits on one line, mark Not tested. Do not choose anyone: close the list by tapping the empty area outside it, or press your tablet's back button.

    You should now see: while the list is open, no name is cut off at the screen edge and the longest name continues on a second line; after you close it, the row is unchanged and Confirm has not appeared.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: you have opened the dropdown on one job row, seen whether the names wrap, and closed it without choosing anyone.

7. Tech: Field Tech Overview

Start here: tap the three-line menu button, tap the gear next to the JobCore© chip, tap Logins, tap Sign out, and tap Sign out again in the question box. Then sign in with your tech login. The app opens on Field Tech, on Overview.

  1. Look at the top title bar.

    You should now see: after signing in with the tech login you landed on Field Tech Overview. At the top, a wide band under the phone's clock and battery row, with a button on each side and, in the middle, one rounded pill with Field Tech on its top line and Overview in smaller white text on a second line directly under it, inside the same pill. The gap between the pill and each side button is narrower than the button itself. There is no separate big Overview heading in the body.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Look at the cards under the title bar.

    You should now see: six cards, one per group, where the gap between the title band and the first card is no taller than the gap between two neighbouring cards: Jobs (counts Assigned and Open), Time Off (Pending and Approved), Reimbursements (Pending, Approved and Rejected), Notes, Photos, Checklist. Also, no small rounded badge floating over the content near the bottom edge and reading Syncing… in grey, a number followed by pending in amber, or Sync failed — tap to retry in red.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: you have checked the Field Tech title bar and counted the six card groups from Jobs to Checklist.

8. Tech: Jobs screen

Start here: signed in with your tech login, on Field Tech. Tap the three-line menu button, then tap Jobs.

  1. Look at the top of the Jobs content and the four corners of the screen.

    You should now see: at the top of the Jobs screen the title band with Field Tech and the menu button, and directly under it the Next Job heading with nothing in between; and no small rounded badge floating over the content near the bottom edge and reading Syncing… in grey, a number followed by pending in amber, or Sync failed — tap to retry in red.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Look at the calendar controls below Next Job.

    You should now see: Week and Month choices, with Month highlighted, and Prev, Today and Next buttons, with a month grid showing. Week, Month, Prev, Today and Next are all on one row; if Prev, Today or Next sits on a separate row, mark Fail.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  3. Scroll down to Previous Jobs, count its rows and type the number in the comment box. If there are no past jobs at all, mark Not tested.

    You should now see: the Previous Jobs list. Exactly 10 rows is a Pass; fewer than 10 is Not tested (you cannot know how many past jobs exist); more than 10 is a Fail.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  4. Tap the top row of Previous Jobs. If step 8.3 was Not tested because there are no past jobs, mark this step Not tested.

    You should now see: the job's own screen, with the words Job Detail in the line at the top, next to a Field Tech link.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  5. In the line at the top of the job's screen, tap the Field Tech link. If step 8.4 was Not tested, mark this step Not tested.

    You should now see: the Jobs screen again, with the Next Job heading.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: you have seen Next Job directly under the Field Tech title band, Week, Month, Prev, Today and Next on one row, the row count of Previous Jobs, the Job Detail screen from its top row, and the Jobs screen again after tapping Field Tech.

9. Tech: Prems

Start here: signed in with your tech login, on Field Tech. Tap the three-line menu button, then tap Prems. You need one prem job showing Claim and one showing Record; if one is missing, mark that step Not tested and do not claim anything yourself.

  1. Find a prem job card that has not been claimed.

    You should now see: a button reading Claim on that card.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Find a prem job card that is already claimed.

    You should now see: a button reading Record on that card.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  3. Tap Record on the claimed job and scroll to the bottom of the form that opens. Do not tap Submit. If there is no claimed job, mark Not tested.

    You should now see: two buttons at the bottom, Save Draft and Submit. The second one reads just Submit, with no longer wording.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  4. Leave the form with the phone's back control, without tapping Submit. If step 9.3 was Not tested, mark this step Not tested.

    You should now see: the Prems list again, with the prem job cards.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: you have seen Claim, Record and the Submit wording, and are back on the Prems list, or have marked each missing one Not tested.

  1. Look at the screen you landed on after signing in, then tap the three-line menu button at the top left, then tap the JobCore© chip at the top of the menu.

    You should now see: after signing in with the sales login you landed on Sales & Marketing Overview, with Overview under the title; then the module list opens with Sales & Marketing as its only row.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Tap the JobCore© chip again so the normal menu returns, and look at the list.

    You should now see: exactly six entries: Overview, Review Library, Pitch Picker, Composer, Reporting, Callback tasks. Overview is highlighted.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

You're done when: you have landed on Overview, seen Sales & Marketing in the module list and counted the six entries from Overview to Callback tasks.

Start here: signed in with your sales login, in Sales & Marketing. Tap the three-line menu button, then tap Overview.

  1. Look at the top of the screen.

    You should now see: exactly one title, Sales & Marketing with Overview under it, and no second Overview title repeated in the body.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.

  2. Look at the content of the Overview screen near the bottom edge.

    You should now see: no small rounded badge floating over the content near the bottom edge and reading Syncing… in grey, a number followed by pending in amber, or Sync failed — tap to retry in red.

    If you see something else: stop, mark Fail, and type what you saw in the comment box.
