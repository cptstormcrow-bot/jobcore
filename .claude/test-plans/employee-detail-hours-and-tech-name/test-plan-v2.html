Screen: Employee Job Detail (EmployeeJobDetailScreen.tsx)

Entry path: Launch app → on the Home screen tap the tile labeled "Admin & Finance" → in the tab bar at the top of the Admin & Finance module tap "Employees" → on the Employee Roster screen tap any employee row (a tech with at least one assigned job, for item 1; a tech with no assigned jobs, for item 2 — if none exists, this item is Not Tested) → on the Employee Detail screen scroll to the "Job History" list and tap a job row.
On-screen anchors: a screen titled "Job Details" (via the header/title area), with an "Assigned Technician" row and a "Back" button.

Screen: Reports Job Detail (ReportsJobDetailScreen.tsx)

Entry path: Launch app → on the Home screen tap the tile labeled "Admin & Finance" → in the tab bar at the top of the Admin & Finance module tap "Reports" → on the Reports screen (banner text "Reports are read-only summaries") tap any job row under one of the status/source groupings (a job with an assigned tech, for item 3; a job with no assigned tech, for item 4 — if none exists, this item is Not Tested).
On-screen anchors: a screen titled "Job Details", with an "Assigned Technician" row, a "Source" row, an "Activity" section below, and a "Back" button.
