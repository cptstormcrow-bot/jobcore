# Tooltips: #0012 Jobs and #0025 Jobs

Draft text for Jeff to edit, 2026-11-02. Nothing is built yet.

**How each tooltip opens:** press and hold the control. A small card shows the tooltip line, and it closes with a tap anywhere.

## #0012 Jobs (Admin & Finance)

| Control | Where | Draft tooltip |
|---|---|---|
| Closed | Top right button | Jobs that are closed out. Open one to review or reopen it. |
| Archived | Top right button | Jobs you archived. They are hidden from this list but not deleted. |
| Add Job | Top right button | Start a new job for a customer. |
| Yellow dot | Right side of a job row | Opens the Dot key below. |
| Green dot | Right side of a job row | Opens the Dot key below. |
| Archive | Right side of a job row | Hide this job from the list. You can find it again under Archived. |
| Detail | Right side of a job row | Open the job's full detail and plan. |

## Dot key (opens from either dot on #0012)

| Dot | Status | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Yellow | Prem completed | The walk-through is done. The estimate can be written. |
| Green | Estimate approved | The customer said yes to the price. The job can be scheduled. |
| No dot | Neither yet | The walk-through hasn't been done. |

## #0025 Jobs (Field Tech)

| Control | Where | Draft tooltip |
|---|---|---|
| Status color and symbol | Left of each job | Opens the Status key below: each letter and color, and the job stage it stands for. |
| Date and time | Assigned Jobs row | When the job is scheduled, or the customer's requested window. |
| Week / Month tabs | Above the calendar | Switch the calendar between one week and the whole month. |
| < | Calendar row, left | Go back one week, or one month in Month view. |
| Today / date | Calendar row, middle | The day the calendar is showing. Tap to jump back to today. |
| > | Calendar row, right | Go forward one week, or one month in Month view. |
| Calendar day | Week or Month calendar | Tap a day to see its jobs and time off. |
| Close | Day popup | Close the day's job list. |

## Status key (opens from the status color and symbol on #0025)

Each job shows a colored square and a letter. The letter is the real signal. Six stages use almost the same navy, and three use grays, so color alone can't tell them apart.

| Symbol | Color | Status | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Navy | Assigned | The office gave this job to you. Accept it so they know you'll do it. |
| Ac | Navy, a shade lighter | Accepted | You said you'll do the job. Next, head out on the scheduled day. |
| ER | Navy | En route | You're on your way to the customer. |
| IP | Navy, a shade darker | In progress | You're on site and the work has started. |
| ! | Yellow | Needs reschedule | The date fell through. The office will set a new one. |
| ✓ | Green | Completed | The work is done. It's waiting on the customer's payment. |
| S | Navy, a shade lighter | Scheduled | The job has a date but no tech yet. |
| N | Gray | New | A new request came in. The office hasn't reviewed it yet. |
| Q | Bright blue | Qualified | The office checked it's a real job. It still needs a date. |
| $ | Navy, a shade darker | Paid | The customer paid. Nothing more for you to do. |
| C | Navy, a shade darker | Closed | The job is finished and filed. |
| ✕ | Red | Declined | The job was turned down and won't go ahead. |
| – | Dark gray | Cancelled | The job was called off. Don't go out. |
| Ar | Gray | Archived | Old job, hidden from the lists but kept on record. |

## Rules (Jeff, 2026-11-02)

- No tooltip on a field that already does a job when tapped, such as the customer name, address, phone, a job row or a Previous Jobs row.
- The "?" in a circle is reserved for the help system. Tooltips never use it.
