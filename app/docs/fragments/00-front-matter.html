JobCore Basics

A working guide to the multi-tenant field-service platform — what's built, what's wired, and what's still a roadmap item.

How to Use This Manual

Each chapter in Part II (The Modules) is written from one of three lenses, chosen to fit what that module actually needs explained:

  • Task lens — walks a specific job the reader is trying to get done, step by step, screen by screen.
  • Feature lens — walks a screen or subsystem's capabilities without assuming a specific task in progress.
  • Informational lens — describes architecture, status, and constraints for content that has no day-in-the-life to walk (Chapter 6 is the clearest example).

Chapters 3 through 5 additionally open with an "A Typical Day" persona walkthrough — a narrative that follows one real role (Admin, Accountant, Service Technician, Sales/Marketing) through the module's real screens in the order a working day actually uses them. These walkthroughs cross-reference the job lifecycle states introduced in Chapter 2 by name, rather than re-explaining the state machine each time.

Note: Training is a roadmap item, not a chapter in this manual — there is no dedicated onboarding/training module or content to document yet.

Badge System

Every feature, screen, and capability referenced in this manual carries one of five status badges:

BadgeMeaning
🟢 Built & LiveWorking, reachable, verified in the app today
🟡 Built, Not WiredCode exists, not yet registered/routed
🟠 Built, UnverifiedCode exists, not yet verified end-to-end
🔴 Not ReachableBuilt but inaccessible from the running app (Chapter 6's status)
⚪ Open Gap (Roadmap)Not built yet; spec'd or implied only
